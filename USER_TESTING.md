# User testing

Goal: watch 5–10 girls use PlayPath, write down exactly what they say, and change the app based on it.
**Only record real quotes from real sessions. Never make one up.** Judges can tell, and it defeats the point.

## Before each session
- Get permission (a parent's, if she's under 18) and tell her you're testing the app, not her.
- Use first names or initials only.
- Open the app on the device she'd actually use (usually a phone).

## Script (about 15 minutes)
1. "Pretend you want to find a team or program. Start from the home page and go wherever you want."
   Don't help. Say "What would you do next?" if she gets stuck.
2. "Find something you could do this week." (dashboard)
3. "Open one program. Would you sign up for it? Why or why not?"
4. "Try the What if? page. What would you change about your situation?"
5. "Change one of your answers." (settings)
6. Ask at the end:
   - "What was confusing?"
   - "What would make you actually use this?"
   - "On a scale of 1–5, how likely are you to show this to a friend?"

While she's doing this, write down where she hesitates, what she taps that doesn't work, and anything she says out loud.
